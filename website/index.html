Questions

How does it read my screenshots?

With a Gemini key, Google's vision models read each image and find every title, including whole rows of posters. Without one, Apple's on-device text recognition does it, free and offline. Either way, every match is shown for a quick check before it's added.

Is my library private?

Yes. Your library lives in a file on your Mac and your keys live in your Keychain. There's no account and no server. The app only talks to TMDB for title details, and to Gemini if you add a key.

Why does macOS warn me the first time I open it?

Binge Library isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm once. Open it, click Done, then go to System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. After that it opens normally.

Which services does it know about?

Netflix, Prime Video, Apple TV, Hulu, HBO Max, Disney+, Peacock and Paramount+, with where-to-watch data for your region from JustWatch via TMDB.

What does "works with AI agents" mean?

Binge Library includes a Model Context Protocol server that only listens on your Mac. Agents such as Claude can search your library, add titles, mark seasons watched and get recommendations, using a key stored in your Keychain.

Does it work on iPhone or Windows?

Not yet. It's a native Mac app for macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.
